# ReceiptRadar: Monthly Executive Expense & Savings Report

### Executive Overview
We audited **5,000 business expense records** representing **Rs.35,016,443.33** in total outlay. Our audit identified **Rs.1,433,026.08** in potential annual cost savings across vendor price arbitrage, category budget spikes, and recurring subscription rationalization.

---

### Finding 1: Same Items Purchased at Different Prices
Our audit found multiple instances where your team bought the exact same item from different stores at widely varying prices:
* **Diwali celebration mithai dabba for team**: You bought this item from **Aggarwal Sweets & Namkeen** at **Rs.4,867.90**, but also paid **Rs.12,320.37** at **Chai Point & Snacks** (+153.1% markup).
* **Impact**: Standardizing this single item to your preferred vendor can recover an estimated **Rs.409,885.51 per year**.

---

### Finding 2: Categories Surging >25% Above Rolling Average
Certain operational budgets experienced severe short-term spending inflation compared to their preceding 3-month baseline:
* **RENT (December 2025)**: Recorded spend of **Rs.721,907.45**, which is **+69.3% higher** than your 3-month rolling baseline of **Rs.426,431.58**.
* **Driver**: Annual lease escalation or multi-facility expansion. Excess outlay: **Rs.295,475.87**.

---

### Finding 3: Recurring Subscriptions & Monthly Services to Review
Recurring services represent a steady cash drain that often escapes review:
* **Municipal trade license fee & challan (Diwali Gift Hampers & Dryfruits)**: Billed at an average of **Rs.4,770.70**, totaling an annual commitment of **Rs.26,238.83**.
* **Action**: Schedule automated calendar reminders for annual trade license renewals to avoid late fee penalties..

---

### 3 Actionable Tips for This Month

1. **Tip 1 (Vendor Consolidation)**: Require your purchasing team to buy all paper, stationery, and packing materials exclusively from **Aggarwal Sweets & Namkeen** to stop paying up to a 153.1% markup at retail stores.
2. **Tip 2 (Budget Guardrail)**: Implement a manager sign-off requirement whenever **rent** expenditures exceed your 3-month average (Rs.426,432) by more than 25%.
